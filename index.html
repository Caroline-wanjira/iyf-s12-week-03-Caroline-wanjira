My Books

The Road Back to me

The Road Back to Me is a deeply personal and thought-provoking journey of healing, resilience, and transformation. It explores the silent battles we fight within ourselves, the weight of expectations, the pain of losing our identity, and the courage it takes to rediscover who we truly are. Rooted in themes of self-worth, emotional growth, faith, and personal awakening, this book is a reflection on life's struggles and the lessons hidden within them. It challenges readers to confront their fears, embrace their imperfections, break free from limiting beliefs, and reclaim their authentic selves. Above all, The Road Back to Me is a reminder that sometimes the greatest journey we will ever undertake is not toward a destination, but back to ourselves guided by bold faith, clear vision, and unstoppable drive.
